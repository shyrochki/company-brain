# Onboarding a new customer

> **Owner:** Oleksandra Riabtseva (planned: Lukas Weber) · **Backup:** unassigned · **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-08

## When to use

When onboarding a new customer: from contract signing to handing the customer over to support.

## Steps

1. Contract signed in HubSpot.
2. Lukas Weber books a 45-minute setup call.
3. Imports the list of venues and staff from the customer's CSV (importer).
4. Configures pay rules for the country.
5. The customer publishes the first shift schedule.
6. After 7 days — a check-in call.
7. Handover to support.

Typical [T2L](../../GLOSSARY.md): 1–3 days.
